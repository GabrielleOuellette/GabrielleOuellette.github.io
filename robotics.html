Combat Robotics Motor Monitor

For Dalhousie's Combat Robotics Team, I worked on the motor and battery monitoring side of the project and designed a custom test motor setup.

My role
The project gave me practical experience connecting CAD design, motor specifications, power requirements and physical testing. It also reinforced my interest in designing hardware that has to work reliably under demanding conditions.

What I worked on

  • Designed the test setup in SolidWorks
  • Created a system to hold a known weight and mimic the ordered motor's demands
  • Researched appropriate power supplies and batteries
  • Considered the relationship between motor performance and test conditions
