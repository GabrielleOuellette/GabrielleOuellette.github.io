Electrical engineering student at Dalhousie University, working toward a Biomedical Engineering Certificate.

I like engineering that ends with a person using the thing. As President and Team Lead of the Tetra Society at Dalhousie, I lead teams that design custom devices for people in the physical disability community, including a catheter belt, a Paralympics device, and a cerebral palsy baby stool.

I have also maintained bioprinters at the Frampton Lab, designed a test motor for Dalhousie’s combat robot in SolidWorks, and researched AI for a business office in Vancouver. Outside of class I organize student events, make content for engineering societies, and work part-time as a barista. I graduated as valedictorian of Fraser Academy in 2024.
