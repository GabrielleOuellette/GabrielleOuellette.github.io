Assistive Technology · Biomedical Design

Tetra Society Devices

Tetra Society of North America · Halifax, NS
2025 — Present

Through Tetra Society, I work with engineering students and volunteers to develop customized assistive devices for people with physical disabilities.

Focus: Create practical, customized solutions based around the specific needs of each client.

My Role

I help coordinate teams, communicate with clients, and contribute to the development of assistive technology projects. The work involves identifying the problem, discussing possible solutions, and building and testing a device around the client's needs.

Engineering Approach

  • Understand the user's specific requirements.
  • Develop practical design ideas.
  • Build and modify prototypes.
  • Test designs with the intended use in mind.
  • Improve the device based on feedback.

Why It Matters

Tetra has strengthened my interest in biomedical engineering because the engineering process directly affects a person's independence and everyday life.
