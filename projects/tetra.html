Assistive Technology · Biomedical Design

Tetra Society Devices

Tetra Society of North America · Halifax, NS
2025 — Present

Through Tetra Society, I work with engineering students and volunteers to develop customized assistive devices for people with physical disabilities.

Focus: As a Team Lead since September 2025, I have had the chance to lead a team to create custom solutions for assistive struggles.

My Role

As President and a Team Lead at Tetra Society at Dalhousie University, I help coordinate teams, communicate with clients, and contribute to the development of assistive technology projects. The work involves identifying the problem, discussing possible solutions, and building and testing a device around the client's needs.

Engineering Approach Given to Each Team Lead

  • Understand the user's specific requirements through client meeting.
  • Develop practical design ideas.
  • Build and modify prototypes.
  • Test designs with the intended use in mind.
  • Improve the device, if needed, based on feedback.

Why It Matters

Tetra Society has deepened my passion for biomedical engineering as it combines my two passions of creativity, designing, and implementing as well as improves people's everyday lives. This organization has also truly opened my eyes to how inaccessible or expensive everyday items or activities can be for someone with a physical disability.
