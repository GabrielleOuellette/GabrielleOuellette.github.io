Combat Robotics Motor Monitor

Dalhousie University · Halifax, NS
January 2025 — April 2026

I designed a motor testing setup around the requirements of a competitive combat robot. The project focused on safely measuring motor performance while working within the electrical and mechanical limits of the system.

Goal: Create a simple test apparatus that could measure motor speed under controlled conditions.

Design

I designed a custom mounting jig in SolidWorks for a Propdrive V2 2836 2200KV outrunner motor. The design accounted for the motor's mounting holes, shaft dimensions and the physical requirements of the test setup.

Testing

The setup was designed around a known-inertia disk and RPM measurement. A gear reduction was incorporated into the system to reach the required testing speed.

Engineering Considerations

  • Motor mounting dimensions
  • Motor shaft size
  • ESC current limits
  • Battery voltage
  • Target RPM
  • Mechanical stability
  • Safety during testing
