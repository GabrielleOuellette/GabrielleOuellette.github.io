Medical Engineering

St. Paul's Hospital Medical Models

Helped replicate wooden medical training models developed and patented by Dr. Roxana Geoffrion.

My role

Woodworked nine wooden models, comprising three models of three different designs. These models simulate a patient during anterior repair, posterior repair and vaginal hysterectomy.

Purpose

The models will be used for medical student training at St. Paul's Hospital, giving students a physical model to work with during their training.

Documentation

Created a step-by-step slideshow guiding the prototype creation process so that the models could be reproduced consistently.

Why this project matters to me

This project was one of my first experiences combining hands-on fabrication with medical education. I particularly enjoyed seeing an engineering design become something that could directly support medical students.
