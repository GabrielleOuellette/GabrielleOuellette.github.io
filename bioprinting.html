Bioprinting Lab Work

As a Bioprinting Technician in Frampton Lab, I supported lab equipment and worked to make printer maintenance more consistent and accessible.

My role
This experience introduced me to the practical side of biomedical research and 3D printing. I particularly enjoyed the hands-on nature of maintaining equipment and documenting procedures clearly for other people to use.

What I worked on

  • Created easy-to-read guides for each printer
  • Documented weekly printer condition
  • Cleaned and maintained 3D printers
  • Worked in a biomedical research environment
