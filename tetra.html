Assistive Technology

Tetra Society Devices

As President and Team Lead with Tetra Society of North America, I help coordinate projects that create customized devices for people in the physical disability community.

My role
This work combines engineering with direct problem solving: understanding a client's needs, organizing a team, developing a practical design and keeping project information accessible. It is also one of the experiences that has strengthened my interest in biomedical engineering and prosthetics.

What I worked on

  • Catheter belt
  • Paralympics-related device
  • Cerebral palsy baby stool
  • Client-specific project planning
